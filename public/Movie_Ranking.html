My Top Movies of All Time According to Himanth

My Top 3 Movies

Saving Private Ryan

This movie is based on World War 2. It starts with with a group of soldiers trying to break through the front lines on the Omaha beach on D-Day. After some time they successfully break through and they are assigned a new mission. that is to bring back home a solider who lost all this 3 brothers in the war.

This movie shows it all. The emotions, fear, pain, happiness, etc. It also tells us about our history and learn from them. I would recommend everyone to watch this.

Jonny English

Its a comedy spy movie where Jonny aka Mr. Bean is an aspiring spy and wants to be on the field catching some bad guys. This movie brings back my the memories of Mr. Bean shows.

This movie is all about never giving up on your dream. Its really a family entertainer.
